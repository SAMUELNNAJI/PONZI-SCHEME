Payments

Paystack configuration — add these URLs to your Paystack dashboard.

Go to Paystack Dashboard → Settings → Developer to add the two URLs below. The webhook is how Paystack confirms payments to your server behind the scenes — once it is connected, deposits are confirmed automatically even if a customer closes the browser after paying.
1 · Webhook URL (required)

Paste this into Settings → Developer → Webhooks → New Webhook URL. Paystack will POST payment events here after every checkout.

{{ webhook_url }}
After saving, click "Send Test Webhook" in the Paystack dashboard — you should see {"status": "success"} returned. Watch the Activity Logs for "via webhook" entries after real payments.
2 · Redirect / Callback URL (recommended)

Paste this into Settings → Developer → Callback URL. Customers are sent back here after paying, and your site verifies the payment with Paystack.

{{ callback_url }}
